  1. Oct 1, 2026 v6.0.0 · Latest

    AI planning and a faster board

    Northwind 6.0 suggests estimates and sprint scope from your past work, and the board now renders 3× faster on large projects.

    Added

    • AI estimates on new issues, based on similar finished work.
    • Sprint planner that flags overcommitted team members.
    • Keyboard shortcut G R to jump to the roadmap.

    Improved

    • Boards with 5 000+ issues load in under a second.
    • Cleaner issue sidebar with collapsible sections.

    The v1 REST API is deprecated and will be turned off on March 31, 2027. Read the migration guide.

  2. Sep 12, 2026 v5.8.0

    Guest access and saved views

    Invite clients and contractors to single projects, and save any filter combination as a shared view.

    Added

    • Free guest seats with access to one project at a time.
    • Saved views, shareable with your team or kept private.

    Fixed

    • Due dates showed in UTC instead of the local time zone.
    • Mentions in comments didn't notify users on mobile.
  3. Aug 28, 2026 v5.7.2

    Bug fixes

    Fixed

    • CSV exports skipped issues in archived sprints.
    • The Slack integration posted duplicate updates.
    • Dark mode contrast of labels in the timeline view.
