  1. Oct 1, 2026

    The Megaphone shoutbox is here!

    v6.0.0 (latest)

    Bassbook 6.0 brings the Megaphone, so you can finally be heard over the drummer, and profiles now load 3× faster. Woohoo!

    Added

    • A Megaphone shoutbox on every profile and group page.
    • A volume slider that only goes up. Nobody can say “turn it down”.
    • Keyboard shortcut G R to jump to the Groove Room.

    Improved

    • Walls with 5 000+ shoutouts load in under a second.
    • Cleaner profile sidebar with a collapsible gear list.

    Bummer: the old Guestbook is retiring and goes quiet on March 31, 2027. Save your guestbook entries.

  2. Sep 12, 2026

    Polls and profile themes

    v5.8.0

    Ask your friends anything (flatwounds or roundwounds?) and dress up your profile with a theme as loud as your low B.

    Added

    • Polls with up to 8 answers. Davie504 already made one: “Who slaps better?”, 8 answers, all of them Davie.
    • Profile themes, from Midnight Fretless to Hot Pink Slap.

    Fixed

    • Gig dates showed in UTC instead of your local time zone.
    • Shoutouts on mobile didn't ping the bassist (classic).
