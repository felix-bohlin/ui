Help corner

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Bassbook, Gold and keeping your riffs safe. Can't find what you're looking for? We'd totally love to help!

Is Bassbook only for bassists?

Yep! Guitarists can visit, but they have to ask nicely and turn it down. Drummers get a free pair of earplugs at the door, easy peasy.

Can I import my old tabs?

Yes. Import tabs, setlists and gig photos from Guitar Pro, your old homepage or a text file. Most bassists are moved over in under an hour.

Is there a mobile app?

Bassbook has apps for iOS and Android with offline tabs, a built-in tuner and a ping whenever someone actually notices your bassline.

Do you have discounts for music schools?

Music schools and garage bands with zero gigs get 50% off Gold. Send us a photo of your rehearsal space and we'll add it to your account.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We take all major cards and direct debit, plus invoices with 30-day terms for bands buying Gold for 10+ members.

Can I switch between Gold and Platinum?

Upgrades kick in right away, woohoo! Downgrades apply at the end of your current billing period.

What happens when I add my bandmates?

New bandmates are added to your next bill, prorated to the day. Fans and roadies are always free.

Where are my riffs stored?

Everything lives in data centers in the EU, encrypted at rest and in transit. Even your drummer can't peek at your diary.

Can my band see my private messages?

Nope! Messages are only for you and the person you're chatting with. Your band can see your public profile, if they ever look at it.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time in your settings. We'll be totally sad, and we'll delete all your data, tabs and pedalboard photos for good.

Still have questions?

Our support bassists usually reply within 2 hours. <3
