Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about plans, billing and keeping your data safe. Can't find what you're looking for? We're happy to help.

How long is the free trial?

Every plan starts with a 14-day trial with all Business features included. No credit card needed, and your workspace stays put if you upgrade later.

Can I import from another tool?

Yes. Import projects, issues and comments from Jira, Trello, Asana or a CSV file. Most teams are fully moved over in under an hour.

Is there a mobile app?

Northwind has native apps for iOS and Android with offline support, push notifications and quick capture from the share sheet.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off any paid plan. Reach out to our team with proof of status and we'll apply it to your account.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, SEPA direct debit and invoices with 30-day terms on yearly plans over €2 000.

Can I change plans at any time?

Upgrades take effect right away and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the end of your current billing period.

What happens when I add more members?

New members are added to your next invoice, prorated to the day. Guests and viewers are always free.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored in data centers in Frankfurt and Stockholm, encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.

Do you support single sign-on?

SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning are available on the Business and Enterprise plans, with support for Okta, Entra ID and Google Workspace.

Are you GDPR and SOC 2 compliant?

Yes. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, sign a DPA with every customer and publish our sub-processors on the trust page.

Still have questions?

Our support team in Stockholm usually replies within 2 hours.
