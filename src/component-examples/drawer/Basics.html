Onboarding emails

Assigned to Lena Ek, due Friday. Marco Rossi reviews the copy, and the design team sends the new header images by Wednesday.

The welcome series sends five emails over the first two weeks. Rewrite them for the new plans, and drop the one about the old desktop app.

Day 1: a welcome from the team, with one button that creates the first project and a short list of what to try next.

Day 2: how to import files from another service, which formats work, and what happens to comments and version history on the way in.

Day 4: the keyboard shortcuts and the command menu, with a short video that shows how to find any file without leaving the keyboard.

Day 7: sharing a project with people outside the team, what guests can see and change, and how to take their access back.

Day 14: a reminder that the trial ends in three days, the price of each plan, and a link to the billing page.

Keep each email under 150 words, with one button and no more than two links. Write the subject lines last, once the body text is final.

Send the drafts to the support team before Thursday. They answer the replies, so they need to know what each email promises.

Test every email in light and dark mode, on a phone and in the web version of the three biggest mail apps, before it goes out.
