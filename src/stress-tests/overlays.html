Overlays inside a dialog

A modal dialog with a menu and submenu, both select kinds, a tooltip and an accordion, plus a second dialog and a drawer stacked on top. Whatever opens last sits above the dialog and its backdrop and is never clipped by the dialog's scroll box.

    • Triggers at the edges

    Menus, tooltips and select pickers on triggers pushed into each corner. Scroll a row against the top or bottom of the viewport and open them: every overlay flips or shifts to stay fully on screen.

    • Flips below when there is no room above
    • Stays inside the viewport at the end edge

    • Submenus near the edges

    Two levels of submenus. Near the inline end of the viewport an inline end submenu flips to the start side, an inline start submenu at the start edge flips to the end side, and no submenu covers the item that opened it.

    • Long content

    A dialog with a long form scrolls inside its own box, a 30 item menu stops at 60% of the viewport and scrolls, a long tooltip wraps at 240px, and pickers with 40 options stay on screen.

    • This tooltip has a lot to say. It keeps going well past the point where a tooltip should stop, so it has to wrap over many lines without leaving the viewport or covering its own trigger.
