The same filled, tonal and outlined groups in left-to-right and in right-to-left text. In left-to-right every group shows a thin line between its buttons. In right-to-left the filled and tonal groups show none: each button draws its line as a box-shadow on its left edge, and in right-to-left the button on its left is the next one in the source, which paints its background over the line. The outlined group keeps its lines, since its buttons have no background to cover them.
