Both callouts close and come back the same way. A is today's Dismissible example: the app's click handler hides the callout, shows a button in its place and moves the focus there. B is a prototype of option B as the package would build it, like opui-copy with --copy: a custom element root that hides itself when a button inside sends command="--dismiss" to it, after a cancelable dismiss event. The close button needs no handler of its own, but the page still listens for dismiss to move the focus, which would otherwise fall to the body, and to remember the choice, here for as long as the page stays open.
