Each row is 14rem wide, with three icon-only buttons and two labelled ones. With .ui-overflow-scroll the icon-only buttons stay 40px squares and the row scrolls. With .ui-overflow-shrink the group sets min-inline-size: 0 on every button, which beats the icon-only button's own minimum width, so the icons give up their width to the labels while keeping their 40px height: in Chromium they end up 2px wide in the control group and about 30px in the toggle group.
