A link card whose header is aria-busy="true" while its release loads. The card link stretches its ::after over the nearest positioned ancestor, tinted blue here, so the whole card is clickable. Today the busy rule gives the header position: relative, so the link's click area shrinks to the header while it loads, and grows back to the card when aria-busy goes away. The spinner itself is an inline-block ::before in the flow and never reads the containing block: the right column sets position: static on the busy header, which is what deleting the rule does, and the spinner looks the same. The readouts give the size of the click area against the card.
