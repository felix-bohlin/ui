Tabs and a hovered toggle button (its hover fill is applied here without the pointer) in light and dark mode, with the default grays and with grays a theme tints toward violet, simulated by setting --ui-surface, --ui-surface-tonal and --ui-text-strong. Today the selected tab in dark mode is white at the hover alpha and the hovered toggle button black or white at it, so they stay neutral when the rest of the theme is tinted. The fix derives both from --ui-text-strong: with the default grays they look almost the same, and with tinted grays they take the tint.
