A valid switch, then an invalid one off and on, next to an invalid checkbox for comparison, in three treatments. Today the invalid switch gets a 2px red halo outside the track (a box shadow the width of the focus ring), and the focus ring moves out by that width; when it's on, the track also fills red. Option A drops the halo and turns the track border red, the hook the track already reads, like Checkbox and Radio color their box and TextField its border; the on track keeps its red fill. Option B does the same with the thick border width, so a 1px red line on a gray track doesn't have to carry the cue alone. In both, the red end text stays.
