A tonal button, a chip and tabs (their default tonal track) in an outlined, a tonal and an elevated card, in light and dark mode. Each card measures the contrast of the button, the chip and the tab track against the card. Today, inside a tonal card the three are the card's own color (1.00:1), and in dark mode the elevated card is the same gray step as the tonal fill, so they vanish there too. The second column simulates option A with two overrides in this page: content of a tonal card reads the page surface as its tonal, and an elevated card in dark mode does too. The fills come back, but in light mode the selected tab, which is the page surface, now matches its track and only its border sets it apart, which is why option A gives the pill a step of its own.
