Account settings
Bassbook Gold: your plan, payment method and past receipts. A change to the plan applies from the next renewal date.
Data export: a ZIP file of your setlists, tabs and comments, with one CSV file per table, sent to your email address.
Notifications: which pokes and gig alerts reach you by email, push or SMS, and whether they arrive at once or in one daily digest.
Profile: your name, photo and the email address people see when you share a setlist with them or mention them in a comment.
Security: your password, two-step verification and the devices signed in to your account, which you can sign out one by one.
