Cold brew is coffee steeped in cold water for half a day or more instead of brewed hot. The slow extraction leaves most of the acid behind, so a glass tastes smooth and a little sweet,␣ even without milk . It needs no special gear, only a jar, a sieve and some patience,␣ skip to inline text .

Guides disagree on almost everything else, but most of them end up where our head roaster does:

Start at a␣ 1:8 ␣ratio of coffee to water, taste it after 18 hours, and only then change one thing at a time.
Lena Handén, head roaster at Kaffebaren

Keep the concentrate in a closed bottle in the fridge, and write the date on the label, like␣ 2026-09-30 . It stays good for about two weeks.

Before you start

Lists

The gear, the steps and today's batch.

You only need a few things:

  • Whole beans, about 125 grams per liter.
  • A large jar or pitcher with a lid, glass if you have one, since plastic takes on the smell of the coffee after a few batches.
  • A fine sieve and a paper filter.
  1. Grind the beans fine. ␣Grind the beans coarse.
  2. Add the cold water and stir.
  3. Steep it in the fridge:
    • 12 hours for a light, tea-like cup.
    • 18 hours for the house strength:
      1. Stir again after six hours.
      2. Taste it at 18 hours:
        • Too weak? Give it two more hours.
  4. Strain it and keep it in the fridge.

Three tips, each with more to say:

  • Use fresh beans.

    Beans roasted in the last month give the most flavor.

    Older beans still work, but they taste flat, and no amount of steeping brings the flavor back. Buy a smaller bag more often instead.

  • Measure by weight.

    Good cold brew takes a day, and no shortcut tastes the same.

How long does cold brew keep?

About two weeks in a closed bottle in the fridge.

Can I drink cold brew hot?

Yes. Warmed gently, it still tastes smoother than coffee brewed hot.

  • Add hot water to the concentrate.
  • Or warm it in a pan, without boiling it.
